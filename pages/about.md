## The Man Behind the Machinery

Greetings, traveller of the aethernet. I am **Mr. Thompson**, engineer, tinkerer, and chronicler of the mechanical age.

I have spent the better part of two decades at the intersection of computation and craftsmanship — coaxing logic from silicon and steam alike. My workshop is a place where code meets copper, where algorithms are as welcome as calipers.

## Disciplines

- **Software Engineering** — distributed systems, backend architecture, Python
- **Mechanical Design** — precision instruments, automata, analogue computing
- **Aetheric Theory** — the study of information as a physical phenomenon

## The Workshop

The workshop is located somewhere between the digital and the tangible. It runs on curiosity, strong tea, and an unhealthy appreciation for Victorian aesthetics applied to modern problems.

If you wish to make contact, the pneumatic mail system is always open.
