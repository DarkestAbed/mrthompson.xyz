## The Man Behind the Machinery

Greetings, traveller of the aethernet. I am **mrthompson**: engineer, tinkerer, and chronicler of the mechanical age.

I have spent the better part of two decades at the intersection of computation and craftsmanship — coaxing logic from silicon and steam alike. My workshop is a place where code meets copper, where algorithms are as welcome as calipers.

## The Workshop

The workshop is located somewhere between the digital and the tangible. It runs on curiosity, obscene amounts of strong coffee, and an unhealthy appreciation for overengineered solutions to modern, simple problems.
